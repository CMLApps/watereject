Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-15. Effective: 2026-09-15.

This is the Privacy Policy for Water Eject & Dust Remover (the "App"), an iPhone application published by Cloud Motion Lab LLC ("we", "our", "us"). It explains what the App collects, why, who processes it, how long it is kept, and the rights you have. If a section is unclear, write to dev@slyapp.co.

The short version. The App has no account and asks for no name or email. The microphone, if you allow it, listens to a one-second chime before and after a run so the App can compare the speaker's level; the audio stays in the iPhone's memory, is never saved to a file, and is never sent anywhere. We receive anonymous usage signals through TelemetryDeck, including the level difference in decibels as a number. Purchases go through Apple. There are no ads, and nothing tracks you across other apps or websites.

The Microphone

The App asks for microphone access the first time you tap Start. You can refuse, and every run still plays.

  • When it listens. Only if you have allowed access, the volume is all the way up, and the sound is coming out of the iPhone's built-in speaker. Then, once just before a run and once just after it, the App plays a one-second reference chime through the speaker and listens for about 1.2 seconds while it plays.
  • What it does with the sound. On the iPhone, each capture is reduced to three numbers: the chime's level at 500 Hz, 1,500 Hz and 3,000 Hz. The App compares the after levels with the before levels and shows one of three sentences on the run's receipt. The captured audio is held in memory only for that calculation. It is never written to a file, never kept after the calculation, and never transmitted.
  • What leaves the device. One anonymous usage signal, micProof, carrying whether access was granted and two numbers: the average level difference in decibels and the difference at 3,000 Hz. No audio, no recording and no speech is ever part of it.
  • You can turn microphone access off at any time in iOS Settings. The level line then disappears from the receipt and nothing else changes.

Attached to every signal by the App: the App's version and build number, the region setting of the device (for example US or BR, taken from iOS settings, not from location), and the number of whole days since the App was first opened.

Added by TelemetryDeck's software: technical context such as the device model, iOS version, the App's language and locale, time zone, screen size and orientation, whether the App came from the App Store, and a session-start signal when the App is opened. No signal carries your name, email, Apple Account, or location.

Purchases

The App offers one one-time in-app purchase, processed by Apple under Apple's Media Services Terms. We never see your card number, billing address, Apple Account, or email. The App reads Apple's signed record on the device to know whether the purchase was made, and whether this device has already used its included first run. Payment data is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Stored on Your Device

  • In the iOS Keychain: whether the included first run has been used, whether a run has ever completed, and which rating requests have already been shown. Keychain entries can remain on the device after the App is deleted, which is why reinstalling does not grant a second included run.
  • In the App's settings (UserDefaults): the last mode you chose, whether the welcome pages are finished, the reason you picked on them, and the date the App was first opened.

None of this is sent to us except as the usage signals listed above.

What the App Does Not Do

  • No account, no login, no email address, no name.
  • No advertising, no advertising SDKs, no advertising identifier (IDFA), and no App Tracking Transparency prompt, because nothing tracks you.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites, and no data brokers.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera, health data, or Bluetooth access.
  • No server operated by us. The tone is generated on the iPhone.

Links Out of the App

After two runs in Water mode, the App can open Apple's support page for a wet iPhone inside an in-app Safari view. That page is Apple's, and Apple's Privacy Policy governs it.

Retention

  • Microphone audio: not retained. It exists in memory for about a second while the level is calculated.
  • On-device settings and Keychain entries: kept until you delete the App; Keychain entries may remain after deletion as described above.
  • Anonymous usage signals: aggregated and retained by TelemetryDeck for up to 24 months in a form not linked to you.

Sale, Sharing, and Global Privacy Control

We do not sell personal information for money or other valuable consideration, and we do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended.
