What the App does and does not do. Read this before anything else.

The App plays a pulsed low tone through the iPhone's bottom speaker. In Water mode the tone is meant to shake water drops out of that speaker's grille; in Dust mode it is meant to shake loose dust sitting on that grille. It acts on the bottom speaker only. It does nothing for the earpiece, the microphones, the charging port or the inside of the device.

The App does not repair, dry or waterproof a device. It does not detect or measure liquid or dust inside a device, and its optional before-and-after level check compares the sound of a short chime, nothing more. A device that has been in liquid may need time to dry, and may need service by Apple or an authorised service provider. Follow Apple's own guidance for a wet iPhone. The App is not a substitute for it.

The tone is designed to be heard and plays loudest with the volume all the way up. Hold the phone speaker-down, away from your ear, while a run plays, and stop the run if the device makes a sound that concerns you.
